# Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards, UPI and digital wallets. Cash on delivery is not available. Gift cards can be combined with any other payment method.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, the order stays pending for 24 hours. You can pay again with a secure payment link from the assistant or the order page. If the order is not paid within 24 hours it is cancelled automatically and no money is taken.

## Card security
We never ask for card numbers, CVV, PINs or one-time codes in chat, email or by phone. Card details are entered only on the secure payment page, and we do not store full card numbers.

## Invoices and receipts
A tax invoice is emailed when the order ships and can be downloaded from the order page at any time. Business customers can add a company name and tax number before placing the order; invoices cannot be changed after shipping.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are valid for 12 months from purchase and can be used across several orders until the balance runs out. Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash or refunded.
