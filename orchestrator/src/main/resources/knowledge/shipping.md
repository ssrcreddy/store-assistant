# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day leave the warehouse the same day.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders over 50 USD; below that it costs 4.99 USD. Express shipping costs 12.99 USD regardless of order value.

## Tracking a parcel
Tracking details are shared by email and on the order page as soon as the order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan after the carrier collects the parcel.

## Missed or failed delivery
If you miss a delivery, the carrier tries again the next business day and leaves a note with options to reschedule or collect from a nearby pickup point. After three failed attempts the parcel returns to us and we refund the order, minus shipping.

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the delivery address while the order is placed or packed, from the order page. Once the order has shipped, contact the carrier using the tracking link to redirect it; redirection is not always possible.

## International shipping
We currently ship only within the country. Orders cannot be sent to international addresses or freight forwarders.
