# Warranty and support

## Warranty coverage
Electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date that covers defects in materials and workmanship. Accidental damage, liquid damage and normal wear such as battery ageing are not covered.

## Making a warranty claim
For a fault after the return window has closed, start a warranty claim from the order page with a description and a short video of the problem. We arrange pickup, and the item is repaired or replaced within 10 business days.

## Extended protection
Extended protection plans add a second year of cover and accidental damage protection. They can be bought only at checkout or within 30 days of delivery, and are non-refundable after 30 days.

## Contacting human support
Our support team is available from 9 am to 9 pm every day. You can ask the assistant to hand you over to a person at any time; complex complaints, legal requests and account security issues are always handled by a person.
