# Refunds

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item passes inspection. Your bank may take a few more days to show the money in your account.

## Refunds for cancelled orders
Cancelled orders that were already paid are refunded within 3 business days. If the payment was still pending when you cancelled, no money was taken and nothing needs to be refunded.

## Shipping fee refunds
Shipping fees are refunded only if the item arrived damaged, defective or wrong. For change-of-mind returns the original shipping fee is not refunded, but the return label is free.

## Refunds to store credit
You can choose store credit instead of a refund to the original payment method. Store credit is issued instantly after inspection, never expires and can be used on any order.

## Charged twice or wrong amount
If you see two charges for one order, one is usually a temporary authorisation hold that your bank releases within 3 to 5 business days. If both charges are still there after 5 business days, contact support with the order number and we will refund the duplicate.
