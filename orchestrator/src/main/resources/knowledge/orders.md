# Orders

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled while they are placed or packed. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled; request a return after delivery instead. Cancelling is done from the order page or by asking the assistant, and you confirm it with a button.

## Changing an order
Items cannot be added to or removed from an order after it is placed. To change items, cancel the order while it is still placed or packed and place a new one.

## Order statuses
Placed means we received the order and payment. Packed means it is ready at the warehouse. Shipped means the carrier has it and tracking is active. Delivered means the carrier confirmed delivery. Cancelled means the order was stopped and any payment is refunded.

## Damaged or wrong items
Report damaged or wrong items within 48 hours of delivery with a photo from the order page. We send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping fees, and you do not need to return the damaged item unless we ask for it.

## Missing items from an order
If a parcel arrives with items missing, check whether the order was split into several shipments on the order page. If nothing else is on the way, report the missing item within 7 days and we will send it or refund it.
