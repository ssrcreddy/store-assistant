# Store policies (sample content for the POC)

## Returns
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Electronics have a 15-day return window and must include all accessories and original packaging. Items marked final sale cannot be returned. Start a return from the order page after delivery.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item passes inspection. Cancelled orders that were already paid are refunded within 3 business days. Shipping fees are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or wrong.

## Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled while they are placed or packed. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled; request a return after delivery instead.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over 50 USD. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Tracking details are shared when the order ships.

## Damaged or wrong items
Report damaged or wrong items within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping fees.

## Payments
We accept cards, UPI and wallets. If a payment fails, the order stays pending for 24 hours and you can pay with a secure payment link. We never ask for card numbers, CVV or one-time codes in chat.
